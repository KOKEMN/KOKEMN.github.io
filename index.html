About me

Studying connected systems in a changing information landscape.

I am a master’s student in Computer Technology based in Chongqing, China. My research centers on network information diffusion dynamics: how information emerges, propagates, competes, and evolves across interconnected systems.

I am particularly drawn to frontier directions in AI agents and AIGC. I want to understand how autonomous and generative systems change who creates information, how it travels, and how digital networks behave.
